## Where this week's numbers come from

- **Weekly-volume baseline** = higher of the last 2 completed weeks' actual running = max(Sep 7-13: **0mi**, Aug 31-Sep 6: **8.72mi**) = **8.72mi**. ×1.30 = **11.34mi ceiling**. Worth naming plainly: this baseline is unusually low because *both* of the last two weeks were non-representative — one absorbed by the charity walk, one by injury caution — not because underlying fitness or tolerance dropped. That's a real edge case in the max-of-2 rule (designed for one down week, not two in a row), but it doesn't matter this week either way, since taper is supposed to be a deliberate volume cut, not a ramp. This week's planned total (**~11.3mi**) sits right at that ceiling, which is a coincidence — it's actually `plan.md`'s own independent ~11mi taper target that's driving the number.
- **Long-run baseline** = longest single run in the last 3 weeks = **9.01mi** (Aug 24). ×1.10 = **9.91mi ceiling** — nowhere close to binding; this week's long run is deliberately well under it (see below).
- **Quality-session count: 1**, per `plan.md`'s Week 6 template — short race-pace reps, not a new progression step. This is sharpening (van den Broek), not building.
- **Parkrun this week: easy jog, no effort** — `plan.md`'s own Week 6 rule (written 2026-08-10, before any of this week's events), and it still fits: no reason to race it 8 days out, especially the week running resumes after a full week off.

## Strength sessions — full detail

**Monday (AM) — Session A, glute & hip stability, ~25-30min, bodyweight only:**
1. Glute bridge — 3×12, 2s pause at the top. *Squeeze glutes, not lower back.*
2. Single-leg glute bridge — 2×10 each side. *Keep hips level, don't let the free side drop.*
3. Clamshells — 2×15 each side. *Slow, controlled, no rocking the pelvis.*
4. Side-lying leg raises — 2×12 each side. *Leg stays in line with the body.*
5. Standing single-leg balance — 2×30s each side (eyes closed last 10s if easy). *Boring on purpose — builds stability under fatigue.*
6. Side plank — 2×20-30s each side. *Hips lifted, straight line shoulder-to-ankle.*

**Thursday (AM) — Session B, quad/patellar control, calf, core, ~25-30min, bodyweight only:**
1. Spanish squat or wall sit — 3×30-40s. *Knees tracking over toes, weight through heels.*
2. Step-downs (low step/stair) — 2×10 each side, slow. *Control the descent — eccentric control protects the knee.*
3. Single-leg calf raise — 3×12 each side. *Full range, pause at the top.*
4. Bird-dog — 2×10 each side. *Opposite arm/leg, keep hips square.*
5. Dead bug — 2×10 each side. *Lower back stays flat throughout.*
6. Standing hip flexor stretch + calf stretch — 30s each side to close out.
